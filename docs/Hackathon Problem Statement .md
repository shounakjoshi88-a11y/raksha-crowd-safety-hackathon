**Hackathon Problem Statements**

The hackathon will feature seven focused problem statements. Each finalist team will select one challenge and develop a practical, technology-driven solution around it.

**1. Trust Gaps in Supply Chain Traceability**

Build a traceability system for pharmaceuticals, luxury goods or perishables that records origin and movement across multiple parties. The solution should strengthen authenticity, transparency and tamper-resistance without depending on a single centralized authority.

**2. Improving Safety at Large Public Events**

Develop an intelligent crowd-safety platform that monitors crowd density and movement, identifies bottlenecks or dangerous congestion, and helps security teams shift from reactive monitoring to proactive intervention.

**3. Security4Her**

Create a technology solution focused on women's safety, digital security, harassment prevention and rapid access to help, while empowering users with practical safety tools and responsible privacy controls.

**4. Virtual Reality for Historical Site Preservation**

Develop an immersive VR experience that allows users to explore deteriorating historical sites digitally. Explore virtual tickets, donations or crowdfunding mechanisms that can contribute towards preservation efforts.

**5. Urban Flood Prediction & Early Warning System**

Predict flood-prone locations using rainfall, terrain and historical data, and issue timely, zone-wise warnings to citizens and authorities. The solution should present risk information in an actionable and easy-to-understand manner.

**6. Smart Elderly Care & Emergency Detection**

Develop a privacy-conscious system that detects falls or prolonged inactivity and alerts a designated caregiver, with escalation when an alert remains unanswered.
